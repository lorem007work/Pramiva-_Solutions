Pramiva Solutions is a comprehensive backend solutions provider serving businesses in developed countries. We specialize in cost-effective, high-quality services including marketing, customer service, sales operations, accounting, and AI automation.

## Brand Guidelines

### Company Name & Logo

**Official Name:** Pramiva Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Trading Name:** Pramiva Solutions  
**Tagline:** Think Bold. Build Smart. Scale Fast.

#### Logo Usage

- Always use the official logo with full color when possible
- Maintain clear space around the logo (no text closer than 10px)
- Never distort, stretch, or alter the logo proportions
- For monochrome use, follow approved black/white versions only

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Teal: #2E8B8B (headings, primary text)
- Green: #4A9B8E (accents, highlights)
- Dark Gray: #333333 (secondary text)

**Secondary Colors:**
- Light Gray: #F5F5F5 (backgrounds)
- White: #FFFFFF (clean backgrounds)
- Black: #000000 (high-contrast text)

### Typography

**Recommended Fonts:**
- Headlines: Montserrat Bold, Open Sans Bold, or Roboto Bold
- Body Text: Open Sans Regular, Roboto Regular (14-16px)
- Maintain readable sizes and adequate contrast ratios for accessibility
