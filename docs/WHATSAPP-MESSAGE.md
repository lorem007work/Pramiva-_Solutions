# WhatsApp Messages to Manager

**Version:** 2.0 — rewritten after internal induction material became available
**Date:** 2026-08-23

The induction material answered a lot. The manager's job is now *correcting drafts and ticking boxes* rather than writing content — a far easier thing to get returned quickly.

But it also surfaced two things that need answering before almost anything else. Those go in a **separate short message sent first**, because burying a domain-blocking question at item 15 of a long list is how it gets missed.

WhatsApp formatting: `*text*` renders **bold**, `_text_` renders _italic_. Keep the asterisks.

---

## MESSAGE 1 — send this first, on its own

Short, urgent, one decision. Do not attach it to the long message.

```
Hello Sir,

Before I go any further I need to check one thing urgently, because it affects the domain name and everything written on the website.

Our logo spells the company *"Pramiva Solutions"*.

But in the induction material the company is called *"Promeva Solutions Private Limited"*.

These are two different spellings. Which one is correct?

If possible, could you send me a photo of the company registration certificate so I can match the website exactly to the legal name?

This matters because:
- We will be buying a domain name, and the wrong spelling means paying again and losing any early Google ranking
- The company email addresses will be built on that domain
- It appears on every page of the website and in Google search results
- If the registered name is Promeva, then our logo has a spelling mistake in it and should be corrected before we launch

I have paused work on anything containing the company name until you confirm.

Thank you.
```

**Why separate:** it is one question with a clear action, and the "our logo may have a typo in it" point deserves to land on its own rather than as item 15 of 23. It is also the message most likely to get an immediate reply, which restarts the conversation.

## MESSAGE 2 — send after Message 1 is answered, or the next day

```
Hello Sir,

I have gone through the induction material and drafted the website content from it. Most of the work is done — I now need your approval before anything goes live publicly.

To make this quick: where my draft is correct, just reply "ok". Where it is wrong, tell me the correct version. Nothing goes on the website without your approval.

*1. IMPORTANT — can we name our client brands publicly?*

The induction material describes our partnership with SNS Multiservices in Australia, and the brands we run behind the scenes — Turf Man, Perth Landscaper, Cleaning Team, Carry or Drag, Hardrex and Public Shed.

This would be the most impressive content on our website. Six live brands across two continents is strong proof of what we can do.

But I want to raise a concern before we use it:

Turf Man and Perth Landscaper present themselves as local Perth businesses. If our website publicly states that we run their operations from Nepal, their customers can find that out. Some customers prefer to buy local, so this could reduce their sales — and they are our client.

There may also be a confidentiality clause in our agreement with SNS that does not allow us to name them.

My recommendation for now is to describe the work *without* naming anyone, like this:

_"We operate six consumer brands across turf supply, landscaping, cleaning, e-commerce, equipment hire and home goods for an Australian partner."_

This shows our capability without exposing anyone. We can always add the names later if SNS agrees in writing.

Please confirm: do you want (a) the anonymous version, or (b) the full brand names? If (b), we should get written permission from SNS first.

*2. COMPANY DESCRIPTION — please correct my draft*

From the induction material I have drafted:

_"The engine room behind growing businesses. We handle the back-office and operations work that keeps a business running, so our clients can put their attention where it earns."_

The phrase "engine room" is from the induction material itself and I think it is excellent — it is memorable and it is not something a generic company would say.

Is this accurate and approved? Please correct the wording if needed.

*3. SERVICES — please confirm yes or no for each*

Digital marketing:
- Search and content marketing
- Social media management
- Paid advertising on Google and Facebook

Operations and customer support:
- Customer system setup and management (CRM)
- Customer service by phone, text and email
- Back-office administration, reporting and operational support

In development:
- AI and automation solutions

Should all of these appear on the website? Please remove any we should not advertise publicly, and correct any wording.

*4. SHOULD WE PUBLISH THESE FACTS?*

The induction material states we were founded in 2025 and have around 10 people.

Both are fine to publish, but I want your decision because it affects how we come across:

- "Founded 2025" tells international clients we are less than a year old. Some will read that as modern and fast-moving. Others may hesitate to trust a new company with their operations.
- Exact team size goes out of date quickly and needs updating constantly.

My recommendation is to leave both out for now. A website does not need a founding year, and nobody notices its absence. Please confirm.

*5. CONTACT DETAILS — needed for every page*

- Which email address should be displayed publicly?
- Which phone number should be displayed publicly?
- What is our full office address in Lalpur? I have the area name but I need the complete address, or tell me to show only "Lalpur, Nepal".
- Which inbox should website enquiries be delivered to? A shared inbox is safer than a personal one so nothing is missed during leave.
- Do we have LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram accounts to link? If not I will leave the icons out — empty links look unprofessional.

*6. WEBSITE STRUCTURE*

I am planning: Home, About, Services, Contact.

I would also like to add a *Careers* page featuring our training academy — the step-by-step training new staff receive. This is genuinely valuable because it works twice: it attracts good candidates in Nepal, and it reassures international clients that our staff are properly trained rather than just hired.

One small thing to check: our academy rules say no AI tools are allowed during assessments, but we also sell AI and automation services. On the same website that can look contradictory. I can add one line explaining that the rule tests individual skill, while AI is a tool we use deliberately in client work. Are you happy with that?

Do you want the Careers page in the first version?

*7. DOMAIN AND HOSTING*

- Do we already own a domain name? If yes, which one? (Please answer my earlier message about the spelling first.)
- Who has access to the domain settings? I do not need any passwords — I will send you the exact records to add.
- Do you want website analytics? I recommend a cookie-free option that shows visitor numbers without needing a cookie consent popup.

*8. LOGO FILE*

Can I please get the logo in SVG or AI format? I currently only have a PNG image. A PNG cannot be resized without going blurry, so at the top of every page it will look slightly soft next to the text. The original file from whoever designed the logo solves this and gives us a clean browser icon.

*9. APPROVALS*

- Do we have any client testimonials I can use?
- Do we have verified numbers we can publish, for example how many clients we support? If not, I will remove that section — I would rather show nothing than a number we cannot back up.
- Who gives final approval on the design, and who on the content? Can be the same person.
- Do we need Privacy Policy and Terms pages? I recommend yes, since the contact form collects personal information.
- What is our target launch date?

*One note on how I am working:* nothing from the induction material goes on the public website without your approval. Everything currently sits marked as a draft and cannot go live by accident.

On costs: the entire technology is free. The only recurring cost is the domain name, roughly ₹1,000 per year.

If it is easier to go through this on a call, I can cover everything in about 20 minutes. Please let me know a suitable time.

Thank you.
[Your Name]
```

## Notes on sending

**Replace `[Your Name]`** and adjust the greeting to how you normally address them.

**Send Message 1 first and wait.** It is one question, it is urgent, and it is the one most likely to get an immediate reply. Sending both together buries it.

**If there is no reply in 2–3 days**, make partial answers easy:

```
Hello Sir, following up on the website questions. If it is easier, could you answer just the company name spelling and the question about naming our client brands? Those two are blocking me. Everything else can come later.
```

**If the reply is "you decide":** that is a usable answer for most items, but *not* for the client brands. Confirm the rest in writing:

```
Understood Sir, thank you. I will proceed with: [list the defaults]. For the client brand names I will use the anonymous version for now, since that one needs SNS's agreement as well. I will share a preview link for your approval before anything goes live.
```

That message converts a verbal delegation into a written record of what was agreed.

### Get these in writing, not verbally

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Company name spelling | Send the registration certificate photo — a domain is not refundable |
| Naming client brands | Potential contract breach; needs SNS's consent too |
| Services list and wording | It is the company's public commercial offer |
| Testimonials, client names, statistics | Published claims carry legal weight |

Everything else is fine to act on from a WhatsApp reply.
