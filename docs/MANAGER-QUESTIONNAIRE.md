## Section F — 🔴 Client brands: may we name them publicly?

### The opportunity

The induction material describes our partnership with **SNS Multiservices (Australia)** and the six consumer brands we run behind the scenes: Turf Man, Perth Landscaper, Cleaning Team, Carry or Drag, Hardrex, Public Shed.

**This would be the strongest content on the entire website.** Six live brands across two continents is real, verifiable proof of capability — worth more than any number of adjectives. It is not being discarded lightly.

### The concern, raised before we use it

**1. It may reduce our client's sales.**
Turf Man and Perth Landscaper present as local Perth businesses. If our website states publicly that their operations run from Nepal, their customers can find that. Some buyers prefer to buy local. Our website could measurably damage the commercial position of the client we depend on.

**2. It may breach our agreement.**
Back-office and outsourcing contracts commonly include a confidentiality clause preventing the supplier from naming the client. Please check the SNS agreement before we publish.

**3. It is not only our decision.**
Even with our full approval, SNS is the party whose brands are being exposed. Their written consent is needed too.

**4. It shows competitors our client list.**
It tells them exactly who to approach.

### Please choose

☐ **Option A — describe without naming** *(recommended for launch)*

> "We operate six consumer brands across turf supply, landscaping, cleaning, e-commerce, equipment hire and home goods for an Australian partner."

Shows the scale and range, exposes nobody, needs no permission from anyone. Can be upgraded to Option B later at any time.

☐ **Option B — full brand names**, after written consent from SNS is obtained
Highest credibility. Requires the confidentiality clause checked and SNS's agreement in writing.

☐ **Option C — leave it off the website entirely**

*Recommended: Option A now, Option B later if SNS agrees. Option A costs nothing and closes no doors.*

### F2. One detail we will not publish either way
